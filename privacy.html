The application may request access to Google Photos only as needed to perform its wallpaper synchronization functions.

Google user data is used only to provide the requested wallpaper synchronization functionality. It is not sold, shared for advertising, or used for unrelated purposes.

Data Storage

Authentication credentials and application data are intended for the user's personal use. Sensitive credentials are not published on this website.

Third Parties

The application may interact with Google services through Google's APIs. Google's own privacy policies and terms also apply to those services.
